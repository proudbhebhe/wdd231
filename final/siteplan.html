Urban Wingz

Site Purpose

The site provides details about the menu and information of this new restaurant, where it's located and how you can order.

Scenarios

scenario1:What options of wing flavors do I have?

Scenario2:Where is the physical shop located?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #1D3D2B (Dark Green)

This color will be used for the much of the text related to the website.

Secondary Color: #E9F6EF (light mint)

This color will be used for background of header, footer and some content in the website.

Typography

Fonts I will use

Bebas, Inter and sans-serif fallback

Bebas will be used in the header, Inter and sans-serif will be applied to all headings and paragraphs.
